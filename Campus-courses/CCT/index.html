About CCT College Dublin

CCT College Dublin (College of Computing Technology) is one of Ireland's leading professional and higher education institutions, founded in 2005. Located in the heart of Dublin city centre at Westmoreland Street, CCT is renowned for its specialist computing, ICT and business programmes delivered at both undergraduate and postgraduate levels.

All programmes are validated by Quality and Qualifications Ireland (QQI) and internationally recognised, with strong industry alignment. CCT's career-focused curriculum, blended learning options, and vibrant international student community — representing over 50 nationalities — make it a leading choice for international postgraduate students in Dublin.

QQI Accreditation

All CCT programmes are validated by Quality and Qualifications Ireland (QQI) — Ireland's national awarding body for further and higher education. CCT's postgraduate degrees are at NFQ Level 9, internationally recognised and accepted by employers and institutions worldwide.

Industry Recognition

CCT holds HECA membership and is part of the Education in Ireland national brand under Enterprise Ireland. The college has strong partnerships with leading technology companies across Dublin's Silicon Docks, giving students direct access to Ireland's booming tech ecosystem.
