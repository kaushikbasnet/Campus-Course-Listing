About NCI

National College of Ireland (NCI) is a dynamic, student-centred institution located in the heart of Dublin's International Financial Services Centre (IFSC). Founded in 1951, NCI has grown into one of Ireland's most forward-thinking colleges, delivering career-focused postgraduate programmes in business, finance, and technology.

NCI's location in Ireland's financial district gives students unrivalled access to multinational employers including Google, Meta, LinkedIn, and Citibank. With a €0 application fee, rolling intakes, and a compact city-centre campus, NCI is ideal for ambitious international professionals looking to fast-track their careers in Europe.

QQI Accreditation

Quality and Qualifications Ireland (QQI) is the state agency responsible for external quality assurance of further and higher education in Ireland. All NCI programmes are accredited at NFQ Level 9 (Masters), ensuring global recognition and transferability of qualifications.

Industry Recognition

NCI's IFSC location puts students at the heart of Europe's financial and tech capital. Graduates gain direct access to multinationals including Google, Meta, Stripe, LinkedIn, and Citibank — many of which have graduate programmes exclusively partnered with NCI.
