About Maynooth University

Maynooth University (MU) is one of Ireland's fastest-growing universities, formally established in 1997 as an autonomous institution. Rooted in a 230-year heritage — the Royal College of St Patrick was founded in 1795 — MU blends rich history with a modern, forward-thinking academic culture set on a stunning campus in County Kildare, just 25km west of Dublin city.

With over 15,000 students from 100+ countries, Maynooth University is recognised globally for excellence in Computer Science, Mathematics, Humanities, Business, and Social Sciences. MU consistently ranks among the top young universities in Europe and is a key partner in Ireland's thriving tech ecosystem, with alumni at Google, Microsoft, Intel, and Amazon.

QS World University Rankings

Maynooth University is ranked #771–780 in QS World University Rankings 2026 — a significant achievement placing it among the world's top universities globally. QS evaluates institutions on academic reputation, employer reputation, faculty/student ratio, international diversity, and research citations.

Times Higher Education (THE)

Ranked #501 in THE World University Rankings 2026, Maynooth University holds the position as Ireland's highest-ranked young university. It consistently tops the THE Young University Rankings for Irish institutions under 50 years old, reflecting outstanding teaching quality and research output.
