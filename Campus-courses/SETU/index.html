About SETU

South East Technological University (SETU) was established in 2022, bringing together Waterford Institute of Technology and Institute of Technology Carlow. With campuses across Waterford, Carlow, and Wexford, SETU is a major force in applied education in Ireland's south-east region.

Serving over 20,000 students, SETU offers a comprehensive range of undergraduate and postgraduate programmes across Business, Computing, Engineering, Science, Arts, Health, and many more disciplines. The university is known for strong industry partnerships and excellent graduate outcomes.
