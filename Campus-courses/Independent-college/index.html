About Independent College Dublin

Independent College Dublin is a dynamic, career-focused private third-level institution located in the heart of Dublin's business and tech district. Founded in 2007, the college specialises in Business, Law, Marketing, Accounting, Finance, and Dispute Resolution at both undergraduate and postgraduate levels.

All programmes are validated by Quality and Qualifications Ireland (QQI). The college is a member of Education in Ireland (Enterprise Ireland) and is renowned for its Law Society FE1 Preparatory Course — the largest and most successful in Ireland. Small class sizes, expert lecturers, and a prime city-centre location at The Steelworks make it a standout choice for international students.

QQI Accreditation

All Independent College Dublin programmes are validated by Quality and Qualifications Ireland (QQI) — Ireland's national awarding body. Postgraduate degrees are awarded at NFQ Level 9 and are internationally recognised, meeting the standards required for professional advancement globally.

Law Society Excellence

Independent College's School of Law is the largest provider in Ireland for Law Society FE1 Preparatory programmes. For four of the past five years, its students have finished 1st overall in the nationwide FE1 examinations — a testament to its world-class legal education.

Entry Requirements

IELTS
Level 8 (UG): Overall 6.0 · Level 9 (PG): Overall 6.5. Equivalent tests including TOEFL, PTE, Duolingo, and Cambridge also accepted.
Pre-Sessional English
4-week Pre-Sessional English programme available for applicants who are one band below the required IELTS score.
Academic Qualification
For MA (PG): Honours degree (NFQ Level 8) or equivalent in law, business, or related discipline. Relevant work experience also considered (APEL).
Documents Required
Academic transcripts, Passport copy, Personal Statement, 2 References, Updated CV, English proficiency certificate, Interview (online)
