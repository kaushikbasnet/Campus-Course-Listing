About MTU

Munster Technological University (MTU) was established in January 2021 through the merger of Cork Institute of Technology (CIT) and IT Tralee. Serving Cork and Kerry — two of Ireland's most economically vibrant and culturally rich counties — MTU is a dynamic, practice-focused university with a proud tradition of applied innovation and industry collaboration.

With over 18,000 students spread across campuses in Cork City, Bishopstown, Tralee, and Killorglin, MTU delivers excellence in Engineering, Science, Business, Computing, Arts, and Tourism. Cork is Ireland's second-largest city and home to a booming tech, pharma, and finance sector, giving MTU graduates unrivalled access to employment opportunities with major global employers.

QS World University Rankings

MTU is actively building its global ranking profile following its 2021 establishment. Its predecessor institution, Cork Institute of Technology, was consistently recognised for applied research quality, strong industry partnerships, and exceptional graduate employment outcomes — a legacy MTU continues to build upon.

Times Higher Education (THE)

MTU is emerging in THE's impact rankings through its applied research in pharmaceutical sciences, engineering, and sustainable technologies. Cork's position as Ireland's pharmaceutical capital — with over 9 of the world's top 10 pharma companies present — gives MTU a unique research and employment ecosystem.
