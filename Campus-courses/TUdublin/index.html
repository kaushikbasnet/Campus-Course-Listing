About TU Dublin

Technological University Dublin (TU Dublin) is Ireland's first and largest Technological University, established in 2019 through the merger of three institutions. With campuses across Dublin at Grangegorman, Blanchardstown, Tallaght, Bolton Street, and Aungier Street, TU Dublin is a powerhouse of applied learning and innovation.

Serving over 28,000 students, TU Dublin offers globally recognised programmes across Engineering, Business, Computing, Creative Arts, Sciences, and Health. The university maintains 800+ industry partnerships, has launched 400+ startups, and delivers a practice-based curriculum that bridges academic excellence with real-world experience.

QS World University Rankings

QS (Quacquarelli Symonds) is the world's leading provider of university performance analytics. Rankings are based on academic reputation, employer reputation, faculty/student ratio, international diversity, and research citations — trusted by students and institutions globally.

Times Higher Education (THE)

THE has provided trusted performance data since 2004, used by students, academics, governments and industry. It evaluates institutions on teaching quality, research output, citations, industry income, and international outlook across 5 pillars.
